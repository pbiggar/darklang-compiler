# Fuse register-coalescing pair collection

Reduce fasta Register Allocation time by eliminating redundant CFG scans and pair sorts.

## Constraints

- Limit changes to pass 5 coalescing preparation and retain one combined pair-collection representation.
- Collect 10 paired alternating before/after -vv runs for every routine program, reporting raw Register Allocation and total timings, medians, and deltas.
- Require identical coalescing inputs, allocated LIR, binaries, outputs, and instruction counts; run the full test suite and routine benchmark verification and report the performance ratio.
- Discard neutral, noisy, or regressive results and remove phase instrumentation.
