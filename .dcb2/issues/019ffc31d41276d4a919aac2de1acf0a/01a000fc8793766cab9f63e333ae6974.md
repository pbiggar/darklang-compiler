## Work item 01a000fc87927e32ae9df8ad1a1a5f82

Title: Replace Float.toString with shortest-roundtrip formatting

Category: behavior

Evidence: C HEAD Float.dark:47-238 caps output at 12 digits and cannot safely handle large finite or nonfinite values; passes/1.6_ValueRendering.fs:246 and 2.6_PrintInsertion.fs:80-84 already centralize rendering through Float.toString. Outcome:

Replace the 12-digit fractional generator with a deterministic binary64 shortest-roundtrip algorithm. Select the canonical closest shortest significand, use interpreter-compatible fixed/scientific thresholds and lowercase e, preserve canonical exponent spelling, append .0 only to finite non-exponential integer forms, and emit -0.0, Infinity, -Infinity, and NaN exactly. Validate every finite output by parsing it back and comparing Float.toBits, including extrema, subnormals, powers-of-ten boundaries, halfway cases, and values requiring 17 digits. Route scalar, list, tuple, record, and diagnostic rendering through the same formatter.
