## How it works

The candidate successfully transforms nsieve's markMultiples from a recursive managed call into a loop backedge, but the registered ANF→MIR verifier measured a regression: 1.0 ms baseline versus 1.5 ms candidate median. The verifier's strict MIR/executable byte-equality gate also rejects this intentional code-shape change, while execution output is equal. No alternative was attempted because this was measurement-only. Residual risk is the narrow Dict alias/cleanup proof; focused ownership and E2E checks pass.

## Algorithm

1. Recognize an ordinary direct self call only when its remaining suffix is cleanup followed by returning the call result.
2. Require exactly one DictHeap decrement.
3. Prove a recursive Dict argument reaches that decremented register through preceding MIR moves.
4. Retain the replacement Dict, preserve its decrement order, move arguments into loop phis, and jump to the loop body.
5. Leave every nonmatching call unchanged.
