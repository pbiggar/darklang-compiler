# Specialize small ephemeral lists for fannkuch

Determine whether an inline small-list representation materially reduces fannkuch reconstruction and allocation traffic.

## Constraints

- Confirm current IR still rebuilds 6–9 element permutation and count lists through take, drop, reverse, append, getAt, and setAt.
- Limit the representation to proven small, monomorphic, non-escaping Int64 lists; preserve ordinary List behavior for every other value.
- Preserve empty-list behavior, bounds behavior, structural sharing, reference-count ownership, and observable List identity.
- Add focused ownership and empty/non-empty E2E cases; run the full suite, routine benchmarks, and focused fannkuch measurements.
- Retain only a material diagnostic improvement or restored full-size completion without meaningful routine regressions; record a rejection with IR and measurement evidence.
- Execution preference: frontier
