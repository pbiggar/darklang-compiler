# Align Blob and cryptography family

Use the interpreter’s immutable Blob across binary encoding, hashing, and certificates.

## Constraints

- Blob scope: empty, length, concat, slice, toHex, fromHex, toBase64, fromBase64, toString, fromString, toList, and fromList; align Blob signatures for Base64.decode/encode/urlEncode and Crypto.sha256/sha384/md5/sha256hmac/sha1hmac; add X509.pemCertificatePublicKey.
- Classify compiler Bytes.create/fromList/get/hexEncode/length/set/toList, Base64.urlDecode, Crypto.sha1/bytesToHex, and every debug-prefixed Crypto declaration as compiler extensions, not Blob parity.
