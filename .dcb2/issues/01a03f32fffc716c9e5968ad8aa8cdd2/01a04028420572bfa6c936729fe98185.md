## Algorithm

1. Identify adjacent or barrier-free scalar HeapStore writes in one MIR block.
2. Match identical SSA addresses and byte offsets.
3. Reject managed values and intervening observable or effectful operations.
4. Remove the earlier overwritten store while retaining the later store.
5. Lower and execute the result, requiring MIR, executable, and output equality.
