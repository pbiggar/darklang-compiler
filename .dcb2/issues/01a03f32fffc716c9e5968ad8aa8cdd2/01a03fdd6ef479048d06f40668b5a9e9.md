## How it works

The failure came from retaining the old store in the reversed scan and dropping the new store. The repair removes only the earlier matching scalar store while retaining the later one. The conservative same-block, same-address, same-offset design was retained because it preserves the intended proof boundaries. The rewrite currently performs a linear scan with local list filtering on replacement; residual risk is limited to broader backend and benchmark validation, which was not authorized during repair.

## Algorithm

1. Scan each MIR block in order.
2. Track scalar HeapStore locations by SSA address and offset.
3. On a later matching store without an intervening barrier, remove the earlier stored instruction and retain the later store.
4. Clear tracked locations at observable reads, calls, allocation, raw memory, reference-count operations, and other barriers.
5. Exclude managed and non-scalar stores.
