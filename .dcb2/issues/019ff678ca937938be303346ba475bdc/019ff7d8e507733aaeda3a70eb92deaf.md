# Fuse MIR→LIR call validation with VReg scanning

Replace separate MIR validation and maximum-register traversals with one per-function preflight fold.

## Constraints

- Scope only to MIR→LIR preflight; retain one fold that validates parameter and call register-bank limits while computing the maximum VReg, preserving deterministic diagnostic ordering.
- Remove all exploratory profiling or instrumentation from retained code.
- Run 10 comparable before and 10 after `-vv` fasta compilations; report raw MIR to LIR timings, medians, and absolute and percentage deltas.
- Because benchmark timings drove selection, report before/after MIR to LIR and total-compilation deltas for every routine benchmark program.
- Run 10 comparable before and 10 after wall-clock executions of `./run-tests --ai`; report raw results, medians, and deltas.
- Require identical LIR, limit-error diagnostics, and generated results, a passing `./run-tests --ai`, `./benchmarks/run_benchmarks.sh --verify routine`, and unchanged instruction counts; reject neutral, noisy, or regressive results.
