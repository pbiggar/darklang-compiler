# Reuse per-function TempId maxima in ANF→MIR

Avoid rescanning every ANF function body to initialize MIR temporary allocation.

## Constraints

- Scope only to ANF→MIR; retain one immutable per-function TempId-maximum summary produced during the existing program scan and consumed during function conversion.
- Remove all exploratory profiling or instrumentation from retained code.
- Run 10 comparable before and 10 after `-vv` fasta compilations; report raw ANF to MIR timings, medians, and absolute and percentage deltas.
- Because benchmark timings drove selection, report before/after ANF to MIR and total-compilation deltas for every routine benchmark program.
- Run 10 comparable before and 10 after wall-clock executions of `./run-tests --ai`; report raw results, medians, and deltas.
- Require identical MIR and generated results, a passing `./run-tests --ai`, `./benchmarks/run_benchmarks.sh --verify routine`, and unchanged instruction counts; reject neutral, noisy, or regressive results.
