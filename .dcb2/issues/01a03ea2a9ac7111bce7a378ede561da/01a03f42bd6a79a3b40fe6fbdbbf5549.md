## How it works

The candidate correctly eliminates duplicate division instructions, but its full edigits runtime measurement is deterministically worse and the registered verifier also reports a MIR→LIR pass-time regression (2.7 ms to 3.0 ms). Alternatives were limited to the preserved candidate versus baseline; the candidate remains available unchanged because behavior is preserved, but it is not a measured performance win. Residual risk is limited to future environments producing different runtime characteristics; this measurement is a clear regression under the requested contract.

## Algorithm

1. Lower the first adjacent signed division normally to obtain the truncating quotient.
2. When the adjacent remainder has identical operands, reuse that quotient.
3. Reconstruct the remainder as dividend minus quotient times divisor with `MSUB`.
4. Retain the existing negative-divisor error path and Euclidean negative-dividend adjustment.
5. Leave standalone division and remainder lowering unchanged.
