## Work item 01a02429a282754f9697067b2bfbe382

Title: Make the parser expose the interpreter operator contract

Category: behavior

Evidence: `src/DarkCompiler/passes/1_Parser.fs:1669-1809` immediately lowers pipes, retains bitwise layers, and rejects comparison chaining; `:1811-1880` gives unary minus higher binding than application; `:2174-2192` only recognizes one-argument `&&` and `||` sections. Outcome:

Replace the public expression operator ladder with the interpreter table: left-associative `|>`, `||`, `&&`, comparisons, additive, and multiplicative operators; right-associative `@` and `^`; application tighter than nonliteral unary minus; and left-associative comparison chaining. Parse every retained parenthesized infix operator as a two-argument function. Parse pipe-infix sections as `left op suppliedRight`. Diagnose `<<`, `>>`, `&`, `|||`, `~~~`, and `!` as reserved unsupported source syntax, without deleting IR/runtime operations used internally.

## Work item 01a02429a2827dffa24448116f9bc732

Title: Canonicalize pipeline and power lowering

Category: behavior

Evidence: `src/DarkCompiler/passes/1_Parser.fs:1680-1705` rewrites pipe RHS through permissive `Apply`; the interpreter’s `toPipeExpr` at `backend/src/LibParser/Parser.fs:794-815` restricts forms. `src/DarkCompiler/passes/2_AST_to_ANF.fs:2647,4039-4075,4519-4526,5411-5421,9359-9369` conflates source `^` with `BitXor`, dispatches `Int` to `Stdlib.Int.power`, and otherwise uses legacy primitive paths. Outcome:

Introduce one validated desugaring path for allowed interpreter pipe segments—function calls, variables, lambdas, and enum constructors—and reject all other RHS expressions. Preserve supplied type arguments, insert the piped value first, and lower partial applications so supplied expressions are evaluated and captured once in source order. Represent `^` as power consistently through type validation and both ANF lowering paths; retain named functions for former bitwise behavior only where the interpreter exposes them.
