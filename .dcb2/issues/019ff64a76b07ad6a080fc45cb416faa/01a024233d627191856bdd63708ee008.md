# Trial result

Reverse-accumulating list-literal bindings improves fasta AST-to-ANF median by 32.9% while preserving ANF, binary, and output.

## Evidence

## What changed

Changed buildSkewListLiteral to cons fixed allocation bindings onto a reverse accumulator and reverse once at completion; added a 64-element list-literal E2E regression case.

## How it works

The prior builder repeatedly appended fixed binding groups to a growing list, copying the full prefix for every leaf, node, digit, and tag. Temporary profiling showed fasta’s 287-element literal emits 1,743 bindings and spends material time in this builder. Reverse accumulation preserves binding order after one final reversal and changes repeated prefix copying into linear accumulation. Residual risk is limited to ordering, covered by identical ANF/binary/output checks and the focused E2E test.

## Algorithm

1. Reverse incoming element bindings. 2. Build leaves, nodes, digits, and tags by consing each fixed binding group in reverse source order. 3. Thread that reverse accumulator through skew-tree construction. 4. Reverse once before returning, restoring exactly the prior ANF binding order.
