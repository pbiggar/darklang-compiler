# Fuse allocator integer and float liveness

Reduce fasta’s current 114.95 ms Register Allocation median by solving integer and float liveness together without changing allocated LIR.

## Constraints

- Limit scope to pass 5 liveness; retain only one combined CFG fixed point sharing classified blocks, successor traversal, and phi-edge lookup while preserving exact integer and float live sets.
- Trial alternatives in an isolated worktree; use temporary phase profiling to confirm both solvers are material, remove all instrumentation, and record and discard neutral, noisy, regressive, or invalid results.
- Collect 10 comparable before and after `-vv` runs for every routine benchmark; report all raw Register Allocation and total-compilation timings, medians, and absolute and percentage deltas.
- Compare 10 before and after `./run-tests --ai` wall-clock runs with raw results, medians, and deltas.
- Require identical allocated LIR, emitted instructions, binaries, benchmark outputs, and instruction counts; pass `./run-tests --ai` and `./benchmarks/run_benchmarks.sh --verify routine`, reporting every count and the `RESULTS.md` performance ratio.
