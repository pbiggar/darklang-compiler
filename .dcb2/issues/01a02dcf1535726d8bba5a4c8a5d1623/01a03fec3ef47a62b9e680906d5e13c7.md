## Evidence

## Important changes

- **Deferred SSA phi-source updates until after dominator traversal** — **Reason:** avoids repeatedly rebuilding successor blocks and replacing the persistent whole-CFG map for each incoming edge.
- **Accumulated renamed blocks and phi substitutions in function-local lists, then materialized maps once** — **Reason:** reduces renameCFG allocation while preserving traversal and ordering semantics.
- **Preserved unreachable blocks and phi instruction/source ordering during final CFG reconstruction** — **Reason:** guarantees deterministic MIR behavior equivalent to the baseline.
- **Removed temporary allocation/time instrumentation from the candidate** — **Reason:** the retained checkpoint contains only the production optimization and focused regression coverage.

## How it works

Profiling recorded 267,928,712 bytes allocated by renameCFG, including 160,527,488 bytes and 292.029ms in updatePhiSourcesForSuccessors out of 483.449ms rename time. The repeated in-traversal CFG-map replacement was therefore rejected in favor of collecting renamed blocks and predecessor-specific phi substitutions and applying them once. Candidate rename allocation was 170,628,512 bytes, down 97,300,200 bytes (36.3%). The registered verifier then measured a clear focused SSA win and identical normalized MIR, binaries, and output. Complexity changes from repeated persistent CFG replacement and successor reconstruction during traversal to one dominator traversal, deferred update collection, map materialization, and one final CFG pass. Residual risk is limited to CFG shapes not represented by edigits or the focused phi-ordering fixture; DCB still owns the full-suite and canonical routine gates.

## Algorithm

1. Traverse the dominator tree using the existing SSA version state. 2. Rename each reachable block without reinserting it into the CFG map. 3. Record successor phi substitutions keyed by successor label, predecessor label, and original VReg. 4. Thread NextVersion and FloatRegs through children while preserving version-stack backtracking. 5. Materialize renamed-block and phi-update maps once after traversal. 6. Map the original CFG once, retaining unreachable blocks and selecting renamed reachable blocks. 7. Apply deferred phi substitutions without changing instruction or predecessor-source order.
