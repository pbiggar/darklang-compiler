## Important changes

## How it works

Baseline allocated LIR and generated ARM64 confirm two dynamic backedge moves after Tak’s three recursive calls: X21 ← X23 and X22 ← X19. The first replacement result is live while the old X21 remains required as arguments to recursive calls two and three; the second is live while old X22 remains required by recursive call three. They therefore cannot share registers. The third result already shares X20 with its loop phi. The allocator already includes every phi pair in both hard compatible coalescing attempts and preference coloring, so adding a preference cannot overcome these interference edges without changing call preservation or corrupting live arguments. No candidate, fixture, benchmark run, or commit was appropriate.

## Algorithm

1. Collect phi source/destination pairs.
2. Attempt merging only when their interference sets do not overlap.
3. Preserve distinct allocations when an old loop-carried value remains live after its replacement result is produced.
4. Resolve the remaining phi edges as parallel moves; Tak retains its two required backedge moves and its existing self-coalesced X20 edge.
