Recommended sequence: first formalize a capability-aware native-effect ABI around the existing ANF/Runtime effects; adopt pure stdlib wrappers plus random/time; then File/Directory/Environment/limited Stdin/Output; then POSIX and process handling. Treat HTTP as a host-runtime subsystem. Keep Matter, package management, SCM, traces, parser/reflection, and CLI-host features out of the initial compiled-application compatibility target: they are interpreter-service APIs and require an explicit embedded-host architecture before implementation.
