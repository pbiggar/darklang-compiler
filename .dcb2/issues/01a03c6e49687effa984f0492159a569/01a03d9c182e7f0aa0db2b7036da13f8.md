## Evidence

## Important changes

- **Fused eligible Int64 `2^k+1` multiplication into unary `Add_shifted` LIR** — **Reason:** enables one ARM64 shifted-register add instead of a shift plus add.
- **Added ARM64 shifted-add and x64 wrapping-`IMUL` lowering** — **Reason:** preserves target-correct wrapping Int64 behavior.
- **Added focused LIR, symbolic, encoding, and generated-code coverage** — **Reason:** proves lowering shape and overflow behavior.

## How it works

The candidate is semantically correct: normalized MIR, executable bytes, and execution output are identical. The retained implementation is a local strength-reduction transformation with no observed behavioral mismatch, but the registered focused measurement on `edigits` regressed from 16.2 ms to 18.9 ms; therefore it does not meet the clear-win requirement and remains available without integration.

## Algorithm

1. Recognize an eligible dead constant used by an Int64 multiply of `2^k + 1`.
2. Replace the constant materialization and multiply with `Add_shifted(destination, source, k)`.
3. On ARM64, emit `ADD source, source, LSL #k`.
4. On x64, emit wrapping immediate `IMUL` for the equivalent constant.
5. Preserve register-use/liveness and classify the new operation as pure for LICM.
