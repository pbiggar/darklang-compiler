# Separate the compiler-only utility surface

Make compiler-only public extensions and internal runtime facilities explicit without presenting them as interpreter parity.

## Constraints

- Assign deliberate public or internal visibility to Stdlib.File.readText, exists, writeText, appendText, delete, setExecutable, and writeFromPtr; Stdlib.Path.combine and tempDir; Stdlib.Platform.isMacOS and isLinux; Stdlib.Random.int64; AWS.urlencode_v0; and Twitter.urlencode_v0.
- Preserve retained extension behavior and document its compiler-specific contract; hide implementation-only facilities from normal source resolution where they are not intended as language APIs.
- Do not rename these APIs into interpreter modules or use similarly purposed Cli, Blob, or package functions as evidence of semantic compatibility. Module-local extensions remain owned by their corresponding parity children.
- Revision evidence: C@51093e0a8e31fe45a9aa79a317fbefd6b74fbcc3 src/DarkCompiler/Stdlib.fs:40-93 and src/DarkCompiler/stdlib/Path.dark, Platform.dark, and AWS.dark; I@04fbe9dcc995c6188757d583e273cbd30a3e2d3d packages/darklang/stdlib has no matching public names.
