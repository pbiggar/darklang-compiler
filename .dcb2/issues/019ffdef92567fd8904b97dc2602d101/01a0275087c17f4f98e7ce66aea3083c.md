# Trial result

Retained an isolated generalized affine-induction candidate with deterministic MIR work reduction and focused correctness coverage.

## Evidence

## What changed

Generalized MIR strength reduction from only 2*i+1 to invariant a*i+b and a*i-b in proven two-block Int64 i+1 loops; added positive, negative, overflow, reused-value, and noncanonical MIR snapshots; added wrapping E2E coverage; updated the optimization backlog.

## How it works

The prior optimizer recomputed invariant-coefficient multiplication each iteration. The retained rewrite initializes the affine value from preheader operands, carries it through a phi, and advances it by a using wrapping Int64 addition. It preserves the original shift-based canonical case, rejects non-unit induction steps, and only accepts invariant operands. Residual risk is limited to the intentionally narrow SSA loop shape.

## Algorithm

1. Prove a two-block counted Int64 loop with i+1 backedge. 2. Recognize a*i+b or a*i-b with invariant a and b. 3. Resolve invariant header phis to their incoming preheader operands. 4. Compute the initial affine value once in the preheader. 5. Replace the repeated expression with a derived header phi. 6. Advance that phi by a after its final latch use.
