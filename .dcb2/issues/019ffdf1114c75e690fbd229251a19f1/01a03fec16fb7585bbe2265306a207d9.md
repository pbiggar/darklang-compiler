# Trial result

Outcome classification corrected: Measurement incomplete.

## Evidence

## What changed

The ANF inliner now processes a call's continuation at the caller depth and only the inlined callee body at depth + 1. This applies uniformly without function-name special cases. Added a regression test proving five sequential eligible calls all inline, and refreshed the ARM64 routine snapshot, RESULTS.md, and HISTORY.md.

## How it works

Sequential calls incorrectly consumed MaxInlineDepth because the old local-call path substituted the callee into the continuation and revisited the combined expression at depth + 1. Name-specific handling and globally raising the depth limit were rejected because they preserve the faulty cost model and make growth less predictable. The retained rule models actual call nesting while preserving existing size, recursion, closure, tail-call, and nesting guards. Traversal remains linear in produced ANF; sequential small calls can grow code linearly, with residual static-size risk bounded here by an 800-byte binary increase and no measured routine regressions.

## Algorithm

1. Check the existing general size, recursion, closure, tail-call, and nesting eligibility rules. 2. Inline eligible calls in the continuation at the unchanged caller depth. 3. Rename parameters and temporaries in the selected callee as before. 4. Recursively process only the callee body at depth + 1. 5. Substitute the callee return into the processed continuation. This gives equivalent sequential sites identical cost decisions while nested expansion remains depth-limited.
