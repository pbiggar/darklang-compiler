## How it works

## Algorithm

1. Identify a nonrecursive, closure-free, tail-call-free tuple-return producer within the size bound. 2. Require that its result is immediately projected exactly once at every tuple index. 3. Inline the producer, remove its returned tuple allocation, and bind each projection directly to the corresponding returned element in ANF order. 4. Retain ordinary tuple-return calls when the result escapes, projections are incomplete, or site limits are exceeded.
