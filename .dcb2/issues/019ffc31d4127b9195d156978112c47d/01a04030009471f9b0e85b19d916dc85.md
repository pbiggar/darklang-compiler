The 1.39% geometric improvement (`0.986089`) is primarily from commit `0809f2b4`, not the final syntax-only commit `a9955d9`.

`0809f2b4` makes integer division/modulus lowering cheaper in `passes/4_MIR_to_LIR.fs`:

- Positive constant divisors/moduli no longer emit impossible runtime-error checks.
- Signed Euclidean modulus replaces five temporary-producing instructions with three instructions that reuse the dead quotient register.
- Dynamic modulus uses one hot-path validity branch; detailed zero-versus-negative reporting is deferred to the cold path.

That lines up with the largest gains in modulus-heavy benchmarks:

- `primes`: 1,893,401 → 1,463,281 instructions (−22.7%). Its trial-division loop executes `n % d` repeatedly.
- `fasta`: 489,035,380 → 477,035,361 (−2.45%).
- `edigits`: 4,478,500,048 → 4,473,890,548 (−0.10%).

Both `fasta` and `edigits` also use `%` heavily with known positive constants. The candidate retains the required failure semantics while removing work that cannot affect those valid benchmark inputs.

The result is not uniformly faster: `matmul` rose 0.95% and `merkletrees` 0.85%, while several workloads were unchanged. The reported improvement is therefore a weighted geometric aggregate driven mostly by the very large `primes` reduction, with smaller gains elsewhere offsetting those regressions.

Finally, `a9955d9` itself changes only the Int power parity-probe syntax and records the refreshed benchmark snapshot; it did not introduce the performance mechanism.
