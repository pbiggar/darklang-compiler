The two regressions are `matmul` (+0.95%) and `merkletrees` (+0.85%). Both stem from the candidate’s required dynamic-shift masking in `passes/4_MIR_to_LIR.fs:605-607,624-629`: each variable shift now emits an extra `AND` before the hardware shift.

`merkletrees` repeatedly evaluates `1 << (depth - 1)` while constructing trees (`benchmarks/problems/merkletrees/dark/main.dark:33`). `matmul` is Dict/HAMT-heavy; its lookups and inserts repeatedly execute dynamic shifts in `stdlib/__HAMT.dark:62-75` for hash chunks and bitmap indexing.

Those workload shift counts are already valid, so the mask adds cost without changing their output. It is nevertheless the mechanism implementing the candidate’s machine-width shift-count semantics generally. No separate correctness issue or integer-wrapper overhead was found; the two localized regressions are the cost of that semantic lowering change.
