## Work item 01a000df09827a959dfe85eb159707df

Title: Native effect and runtime-state boundary

Category: other

## Work item 01a000df09827fe991b22ae7cade1140

Category: behavior

Outcome:

Provide target-specific process and host primitives for macOS ARM64, Linux ARM64, and Linux x86_64: environment access, PATH-aware direct execution, pipes, descriptor duplication/closure/nonblocking mode, concurrent stdout/stderr draining, wait-status normalization, signals, timeout clocks, sleep, uname, PID/UID, account lookup, online CPU count, TTY detection, termios, polling, ioctl, and SIGWINCH. Use the native syscall or platform ABI boundary, preserve errno and platform messages, retry interruptible operations where the interpreter runtime does, and close every descriptor on success and failure.
