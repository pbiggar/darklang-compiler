# Trial result

CSE was the largest profiled MIR subpass, but its no-op fast-path trial had no reliable improvement under comparable edigits measurements.

## Evidence

## What changed

No changes retained or committed; temporary exclusive-subpass profiling and the CSE no-expression guard plus focused E2E case were removed.

## How it works

Exclusive profiling across 6,760 fixed-point iterations ranked CSE at 614.110ms and DCE at 541.791ms; DCE was excluded because scan fusion had already been rejected. The CSE trial skipped dominator construction when no BinOp or UnaryOp exists, preserving optimizer order and output, but selected-pass samples were regressive/noisy and the interleaved control pair was heavily load-affected, so no candidate was retained.

## Algorithm

Trial only: scan CFG instructions for CSE-eligible BinOp or UnaryOp; return unchanged CFG when absent; otherwise run the existing CSE algorithm unchanged. The trial was removed.
