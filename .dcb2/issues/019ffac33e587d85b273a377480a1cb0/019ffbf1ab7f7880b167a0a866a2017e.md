# Design brief

## Problem

At compiler HEAD 51093e0a8e31fe45a9aa79a317fbefd6b74fbcc3 versus darklang/dark 04fbe9dcc995c6188757d583e273cbd30a3e2d3d, optional/result control flow and retry support have observable parity gaps in branch selection, callback-result propagation, and delay sequencing.

## Goals

- Match the interpreter’s public behavior at the pinned revisions.
- Preserve callback results, termination behavior, and fixed or progressive delays.
- Classify compiler-only extensions and intentional divergences explicitly.

## Proposed solution

Complete the optional/result composition surface and introduce one coherent retry model whose variants differ only by termination and delay policy. Use interpreter behavior as the baseline, preserve callback results unchanged, and document any compiler-specific behavior separately.

## Success criteria

- Equivalent source produces matching empty, success, and failure outcomes across both revisions.
- Retries invoke callbacks, terminate or continue, return results, and delay in the same observable order as the interpreter.
- Parity evidence pins every comparison to compiler 51093e0a8e31fe45a9aa79a317fbefd6b74fbcc3 and darklang/dark 04fbe9dcc995c6188757d583e273cbd30a3e2d3d and labels all extensions or intentional divergences.
