# Transfer ownership through recursive-sum construction

Remove redundant reference-count traffic when freshly built tree children are immediately assembled into a recursive sum.

## Constraints

- Reconfirm current binary_trees LIR still increments both child results, increments the tuple payload, then decrements all three values.
- Restrict the transform to freshly owned, non-escaping aggregate results whose ownership is transferred exactly once.
- Preserve recursive-sum destruction, aliasing, allocation failure behavior, and ARM64/x64 parity.
- Require focused IR and allocation-count evidence, ./run-tests --ai, and ./benchmarks/run_benchmarks.sh --verify routine; retain only a measurable gain without routine regressions.
- Record a rejected result if ownership proof or measurement is inconclusive.
