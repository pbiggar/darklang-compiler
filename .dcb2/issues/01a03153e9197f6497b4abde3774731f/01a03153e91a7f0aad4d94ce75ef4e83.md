# Unswitch invariant scalar loop branches

Remove repeated loop branches when their Boolean condition is proven invariant for the entire natural loop.

## Constraints

- Limit the initial transform to reducible natural loops with a unique preheader and pure scalar loop bodies.
- Preserve loop entry, exit, phi, reference-count, and call behavior; exclude memory, allocation, ownership, and effectful conditions.
- Require failing MIR/LIR and generated-code tests for true, false, changing-condition, and multi-exit cases.
- Retain only measured gains; run ./run-tests --ai and ./benchmarks/run_benchmarks.sh --verify routine, update docs/classic-optimizations.md, and remove temporary benchmark inputs.
