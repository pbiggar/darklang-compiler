# Design brief

## Problem

At compiler HEAD 51093e0a8e31fe45a9aa79a317fbefd6b74fbcc3, compared with darklang/dark 04fbe9dcc995c6188757d583e273cbd30a3e2d3d, two public value-inspection utility packages are absent, preventing interpreter-parity use in compiled programs.

## Goals

- Provide the complete scoped utility surfaces with the interpreter’s public behavior as the baseline.
- Tie parity evidence to the exact compiler and interpreter revisions.
- Classify any compiler-only extensions or intentional divergences explicitly.

## Proposed solution

Add both utilities as one coherent standard-library capability, preserving their related public types and observable semantics from the pinned interpreter revision. Validate behavior through focused same-source comparisons; treat any necessary departure as an explicit, documented divergence rather than an implicit fallback. No existing compiler-only extension or intentional divergence is established by the current comparison.

## Success criteria

- All scoped utility behavior is available to compiled programs.
- Representative same-source programs produce equivalent observable results in the compiler and pinned interpreter.
- Every comparison records both exact revisions, and any remaining behavioral difference is explicitly classified.
