# Classify compiler-only utility surface

Define which compiler-only utility APIs remain extensions and which become private.

## Constraints

- Classify Stdlib.File.readText/exists/writeText/appendText/delete/setExecutable/writeFromPtr; Stdlib.Path.combine/tempDir; Stdlib.Platform.isMacOS/isLinux; Stdlib.Random.int64; AWS.urlencode_v0; Twitter.urlencode_v0.
- Do not treat similarly purposed interpreter Cli, Blob, or package functions as parity for these compiler names; module-specific aliases and extensions are owned by their corresponding proposals above.
- Evidence: C@51093e0a8e31fe45a9aa79a317fbefd6b74fbcc3 src/DarkCompiler/Stdlib.fs:40-93 and stdlib/{Path,Platform,AWS}.dark; I@04fbe9dcc995c6188757d583e273cbd30a3e2d3d packages/darklang/stdlib has no matching public modules or names.
