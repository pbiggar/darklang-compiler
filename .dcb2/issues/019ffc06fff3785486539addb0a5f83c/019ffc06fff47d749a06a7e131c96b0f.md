# Trial the highest-evidence compiler-pass hot path

Retain one clear, non-noisy compile-time optimization in the highest-cost eligible pass.

## Constraints

- Use fresh -vv timings and profiling to select exactly one pass or hot path; exclude hypotheses already recorded in .dcb2.
- Run all experiments in one isolated worktree at a fixed compiler revision, with identical benchmark inputs, command order, and host conditions.
- Record raw before and after measurements, medians, absolute deltas, and percentage deltas.
- When benchmarks drive selection, report selected-pass and total-compilation deltas for every benchmark program.
- Compare 10 before and 10 after ./run-tests --ai wall-clock runs and retain only a stable improvement exceeding observed noise.
- Run ./run-tests --ai and ./benchmarks/run_benchmarks.sh --verify routine; preserve behavior and instruction counts unless separately justified.
- Retain at most one optimization and remove all exploratory profiling and instrumentation; record and discard neutral, noisy, regressive, or invalid alternatives.
