# Design brief

## Problem

At compiler HEAD 51093e0a8e31fe45a9aa79a317fbefd6b74fbcc3, streams are absent, preventing parity with interpreter revision 04fbe9dcc995c6188757d583e273cbd30a3e2d3d and its observable lifecycle semantics.

## Goals

- Provide the full requested lazy stream surface.
- Match deterministic single-consumer, exhaustion, error, closure, and disposal behavior.
- Keep portable behavior shared while isolating native resource management and explicitly documenting extensions or intentional divergences.

## Proposed solution

Introduce an opaque, resource-backed stream abstraction whose state transitions enforce one consumer and deterministic cleanup. Implement derivable transformations portably, retaining advancement, closure, and disposal at the native boundary. Maintain a comparison ledger anchored to the exact revisions above, using DCB1 report 8a402797 only as an initial lead and supporting retained findings with current source evidence and focused same-source probes.

## Success criteria

- Equivalent programs have matching values, errors, and lifecycle behavior against the pinned interpreter revision.
- Repeated consumption, exhaustion, callback failure, early termination, and explicit closure produce deterministic outcomes.
- Closure is idempotent and resources are disposed after success, failure, exhaustion, early termination, or explicit closure.
- Compiler-only extensions and intentional divergences are clearly separated from parity claims.
