## How it works

The RC pass lacked cleanup for a retain immediately followed by its corresponding release. The retained O(n) ANF traversal removes only exact adjacent pairs after RC insertion. Broader cancellation was rejected because intervening bindings or control flow could represent uses, calls, allocations, aliases, transfers, final releases, or runtime-error boundaries. Fixed roots require identical value, payload size, root kind, and metadata; dynamic strings and blobs require matching operation kinds and values. The new baseline’s candidate-related compiler and test paths are identical to those used for the prior pinned-baseline measurement, making the recovered two-to-zero comparison directly applicable. Residual risk is limited to the invariant that a valid retain operand is already live; the conservative matcher intentionally misses non-adjacent opportunities.

## Algorithm

1. Traverse each function and main ANF expression after reference-count insertion.
2. Inspect each pair of immediately nested Let bindings.
3. Recognize retain followed by the corresponding release.
4. Require the same atom and matching dynamic-buffer operation, or identical fixed-root size, kind, and metadata.
5. Remove both bindings and continue recursively through their body.
6. Traverse branch bodies independently without matching across a branch.
7. Preserve every sequence containing an intervening binding or any shape/value mismatch.
