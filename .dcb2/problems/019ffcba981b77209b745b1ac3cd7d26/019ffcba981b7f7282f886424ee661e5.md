# Improve generated-code performance from benchmarks

Use current benchmark results, IR, assembly, and `docs/investigations/benchmark-*.md` to find narrow compiler or runtime optimization opportunities. Investigate one benchmark and one candidate at a time.

First confirm the candidate is still relevant and not already implemented. Then test it in isolation with focused transformation evidence, the full test suite, and routine benchmarks. Keep only a defensible improvement without meaningful regressions; remove completed candidates from active notes and record rejected experiments with their evidence.
