# Optimize compiler pass compile time

Continuously find measurable compiler-pass compile-time improvements. Generate short trial-first issues, each limited to one pass or hot path and one retained optimization. Select candidates from current `-vv` timing or profiling evidence, vary selection across eligible passes, and do not favor an easy-looking pass without performance evidence.

Every trial must:

- establish comparable before and after measurements with raw results, medians, and deltas;
- report selected-pass and total-compilation deltas for every benchmark program when benchmark evidence drove selection;
- compare 10 before and after `./run-tests --ai` wall-clock runs;
- preserve correctness, benchmark behavior, and instruction counts unless a difference is separately justified;
- remove exploratory profiling and instrumentation from retained code.

Try alternatives in an isolated worktree. Keep only a clear, non-noisy improvement that passes verification. Record and discard neutral, noisy, regressive, or invalid results.
