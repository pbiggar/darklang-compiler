# Optimize compiler pass compile time

Continuously find measurable compiler-pass compile-time bottlenecks and propose short, independently reviewable optimization issues. Select passes from current `-vv` timing or profiling evidence, vary selection across eligible passes, and do not favor an easy-looking pass without performance evidence.

Every proposed issue must be limited to one pass or hot path and one retained optimization. Its constraints must require:

- 10 comparable before and after selected-pass timing runs, with raw results, medians, and deltas;
- selected-pass and total-compilation deltas for every benchmark program when benchmark evidence drove selection;
- 10 comparable before and after `./run-tests --ai` wall-clock runs;
- correctness, benchmark, and instruction-count validation when the changed pass can affect them;
- removal of exploratory profiling and instrumentation from retained code.

Analysis may inspect, measure, profile, and try alternatives without proposing code for integration. Findings become proposed issues and require individual human approval before implementation. Neutral or noisy results are not improvements.
