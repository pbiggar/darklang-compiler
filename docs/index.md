The tables above are the retained index tables. `RESULTS.md` and `BASELINES.md`
are the only generated documentation tables; their producers are stated above.
Feature and compatibility tables are maintained from their named source and
must not be updated from a historical observation alone.

## Operational data

`agents/*/agent.json` and the executable tools they name are operational data,
not contributor documentation. Their human-readable entry point is this index
and `AGENTS.md`. Historical investigations, journals, and duplicate playbooks
are intentionally not retained as current documentation.
